Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Aropas (the "App") handles your information.

1. Camera Data & AI Processing

The App uses your device's camera for hand gesture and body posture recognition. All image processing is performed locally on your device using artificial intelligence technologies. No camera frames, videos, or facial data are ever uploaded to any server, stored permanently, or shared with third parties.

2. Local Storage

The App saves "Play History" images (screenshots of matches) to your device's internal storage. These images remain solely on your device. You can view or delete these images at any time using the "Check" feature in the App.

3. Advertising

The App uses third-party advertising services to support development:

  • Google AdMob: Used on devices with Google Play Services.
  • Huawei Petal Ads: Used on Huawei devices without Google Play Services.

These providers may collect and use non-personal data, such as your Advertising ID, to show relevant ads. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for more information.

4. Permissions

The App requires the following permissions to function:

  • Camera: To recognize your gestures/postures.
  • Storage: To save and load your play history and custom music/avatars.

5. Contact

If you have any questions, please contact us at: aropas.play@gmail.com
