1. Introduction

Aropas (Active Rock-Paper-Scissors) is a traditional game reinvented with a dynamic, physical twist. Instead of just using your fingers, you play this version also with your entire body. By striking different full-body postures to represent rock, paper, or scissors, you actively stretch and strain the muscles and joints in your arms, legs, and torso, while your hand movements help relax your fingers and wrists.

This makes Aropas the perfect way to break up long hours of sitting in a fixed position, whether you’ve been working at a computer or staring at a phone. To play, simply set your phone down and perform gestures over the screen, or prop your phone up vertically, step back into the camera’s field of view, and strike your poses against the AI. Winning or losing doesn’t matter—the real victory is getting your body moving, stretching, and taking a well-deserved break from your screen.

Body Rock-Paper-Scissors is played just like the universally popular hand-based version. Numerous examples reflecting its real-world popularity can be found across the web. For instance, Prof. Dr. Christian Andrä, an expert in the field of Movement and Sports Pedagogy, has demonstrated a version of body Rock-Paper-Scissors alongside his students on his YouTube channel, highlighting its health and fitness benefits for both the young and the old.

Traditionally, hand / body Rock-Paper-Scissors require a partner standing directly in front of you. To overcome this limitation, Aropas leverages modern technology, specifically your smartphone and Artificial Intelligence (AI). Your phone acts as an interactive opponent by using its camera to recognize your live gestures and body postures, allowing you to enjoy the full win, loss, or draw experience entirely on your own.

Moreover, modern work and leisure routines often leave us in static postures in front of screens for extended periods, frequently leading to physical discomfort and long-term health issues. Aropas offers the ideal active break from sedentary screen time to help you rebalance your daily routine. By stepping away from your device for just a moment, you can actively stretch, move, and fully relieve tension across your muscles, joints, and body.

2. Features of Aropas

The game features four main tabs—Auto Play, Active Play, Active Body Play, and General Settings—which you can easily navigate using the arrow indicators in the top bar:

• Auto Play: Watch two AI players battle it out directly on your phone. Depending on your preference, you can choose Full Auto or Half Auto mode with a single click or multiple clicks of the play button.

A play after button 'Play' clicked, yellow indicates 'Draw' for this play, this the 1st play(R1)
A play after button 'Play' clicked, red / green indicates 'lost'/'win' for the 2nd play (R2)
Result of this round, yellow indicates draw for this round as whole with text overlapped
view after 'check' clicked, here is the 3rd play (R3), the R1, R2 also recored, click left/right arrow to browser

• Active Play: Face off against your phone! In this mode, your phone is Player 1 and you are Player 2. Depending on the selected mode, you can either tap the gesture icons on the screen or show your real hand gestures directly to the open camera.

• Active Body Play: It’s you vs. your phone in a full-body challenge. Choose whether to play by tapping the posture icons or by stepping back to strike real physical poses in front of your phone's camera.

Tailor your workout: The physical intensity of these three modes scales from light to heavy—ranging from a simple button click to full-body movement. You decide exactly how much you want to move!

• General Settings: In this final section, you can fully customize your experience. Set player names, choose profile photos, select countdown voices, pick your favorite victory music, and change the app language to your liking.
