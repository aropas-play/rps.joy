2.1 in the link from https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=act.joy.rps&hl=en-US&ah=Y4muNObUts-jCr9_ZqqhPQIZc8E
    the button "install on more device" call up a pop up windows, in which under combibox, it states: the app access to location, phone status, microphone, Wi-Fi connection information, and others,
    which are more than I declared, why?

    The extra permissions shown on Google Play (like Location, Phone Status, Microphone, and Wi-Fi connection info) are caused by Manifest Merging from Third-Party Libraries.
    1. Why Are Extra Permissions Included?
    When you build your Android App (.aab or .apk), Gradle uses the Manifest Merger tool. It combines your main AndroidManifest.xml with the hidden AndroidManifest.xml files inside every 3rd-party library/SDK in your project:
    •    Google AdMob & Huawei Petal Ads: These ad networks automatically include permissions to optimize ad delivery and fraud detection:
    ◦    Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): For location-targeted ads.
    ◦    Phone Status (READ_PHONE_STATE): For device identification on older Android versions.
    ◦    Wi-Fi Information (ACCESS_WIFI_STATE): To check network connection type.
    •    Camera / AI / ML SDKs (e.g., CameraX, ML Kit, MediaPipe): Certain camera or audio/video processing libraries include RECORD_AUDIO (Microphone) by default for video recording features.
    2. How to Inspect Which Library Added Each Permission
    You can check exactly which library added each permission inside Android Studio:
    2.1.    Open AndroidManifest.xml in Android Studio.
    2.2.    Click the Merged Manifest tab at the bottom of the code editor window.
    2.3.    Look through the merged permissions list. On the right panel, Android Studio will show the exact library (e.g., com.google.android.gms:play-services-ads) that declared each permission.
    3. How to Block/Remove Unwanted Permissions
    If a 3rd-party SDK merged permissions that your app does not actually need, you can explicitly block and remove them in your main AndroidManifest.xml using tools:node="remove":
    <manifest xmlns:android="http://schemas.android.com/apk/res/android"
        xmlns:tools="http://schemas.android.com/tools">
        <!-- Explicitly remove permissions merged from third-party SDKs -->
        <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION" tools:node="remove" />
        <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION" tools:node="remove" />
        <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.READ_PHONE_STATE" tools:node="remove" />
        <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO" tools:node="remove" />
        <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE" tools:node="remove" />
    </manifest>
    4. Next Steps
    4.1.    Add tools:node="remove" for any permissions you do not want in your app.
    4.2.    Build a new App Bundle (.aab) and upload it to Google Play Console.
    4.3.    Update the Data Safety questionnaire in Google Play Console to match your updated permissions.

    •CameraX / ML Kit / MediaPipe:
        ◦For real-time hand gesture and body posture recognition, only <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.CAMERA" /> is required.
        ◦Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) is only needed if recording video with sound, which Aropas does not require.

EmailJS lets you send emails directly from your JavaScript code in the background using emailjs.send().

i. You create a free account on EmailJS and connect your Gmail (aropas.play@gmail.com).
ii. Include the EmailJS SDK script tag in your HTML.
iii. Replace window.location.href = "mailto:..." with emailjs.send(...).
